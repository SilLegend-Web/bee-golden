Help

How saving works

Your changes are not live until you press Save changes. After saving, refresh the website to see them.

Adding a product

Press + Add product, fill in the name, category and price, choose a photo and press Done, then Save changes. A plain background looks best.

Hero section

The hero shows pieces with no background. For each hero piece, upload a transparent image (PNG or WebP with the background removed) in the product's editor. Free tools such as remove.bg can do this. Without one, the hero shows the normal photo with its background.

Runway

Runway pieces turn 360° only if they have a 360° turntable image. Pieces without one still appear in a gold frame, as a normal photo. Turntable images are built from a short video that goes once around the piece. Send the video to Claude, who will prepare the turntable image for you to upload under Advanced in the product editor.

Prices and payments

The payment system reads the prices you save here, so what customers pay always matches the shop. If you mark a product Sold out or Hide it, it cannot be bought.

Lost or stolen password

In Netlify, open Site configuration, then Environment variables, change ADMIN_PASSWORD and deploy again. Everyone who is logged in is logged out.
