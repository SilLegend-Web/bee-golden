Bee Golden Collections is a fashion shop with two sides. We sew African clothing, from dashikis and agbada sets to tailored jackets, cut and finished to your measurements. We also stock quality ready-made clothes you can take home and wear today.

Our clothes are for everyone: women, men and children. Gold detailing, rich African patterns and clean finishing run through what we make, and that is where our name comes from.
